Drone Motor Design

As product designer for four types of drone motor, I learned what it truly takes to bring a design to mass production: complete the mechanical design, craft a golden sample, draft the assembly SOP, and validate the product against it — every step written down like a recipe worth repeating.

PX4 Autopilot

Development of a flight control system built on the open-source PX4 framework for multirotor drones. I implemented flight-path planning through the ground control station, achieving autonomous takeoff, mission execution, and return-to-home — the aircraft flying its own calligraphy across the sky.

Firmware · 無感測控制

Motor Control Firmware

A sensorless zero-crossing detection control program for brushless DC motors, developed from scratch. I worked closely with the client to define specifications, use cases, and action flow — so the program's logic reads as clearly as its requirements. On the mechanical side, I handled the supporting sheet-metal design.

Thermal Design · 水冷

Water-Cooled Driver Enclosure

A water-cooled enclosure for motor drivers. I proposed the implementation plan — waterproofing strategy and communication methods included — then completed the mechanical design and validated the finished product.
