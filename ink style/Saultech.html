Machines measured in microns

In the Semiconductor Development Department I designed equipment where a single particle matters — including a world-first machine that now carries my patents inside it.

World First · 潔淨

Semiconductor Die Cleaning Machine

The world's first semiconductor die cleaning machine. I completed the mechanism and process-layout design independently, verified every key module through experiment, and filed patents for the inventions along the way — then coordinated across departments to bring the finished machine to life.

Optical Inspection · 檢測

Bonding Inspection Machine

A hybrid-bonding inspection machine that shines infrared light through silicon to reveal the quality of a bond. The adjacent tensile-testing module measures the force needed to separate bonded chips and wafers. I handled the mechanical design, optical component selection, assembly, and electrical distribution.

Precision Optics · 微粒

Particle Detection Module

A module that finds particles on wafers and chips by catching laser light scattered from their surfaces with a photomultiplier tube. I selected the optics, carried out the mechanical design, and validated the module end to end.

UV Debonding Machine

A compact UV debonding machine, designed and assembled entirely by my own hands — proof that even the simplest machine deserves a considered design.

The Hybrid Bonder in Motion

I created the demonstration animation for this hybrid bonder — shown at the company's semiconductor exhibition booth.
