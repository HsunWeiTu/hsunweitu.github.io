My career began while I was still pursuing my master's degree at National Taiwan University. Since then, three companies have shaped my craft — each one a new chapter in the same scroll.

Chapter One · Biomedical & Pet-Care Tech

Techncare Biomedical Technology

My first professional chapter spanned patents, product research, clinical trials, and hands-on engineering:

  1. Patent Application & Analysis — Collaborated with TOP TEAM International Patent and Trademark Office to file two company patents, and assessed infringement risk for company products through targeted patent research.
  2. Product White Paper Research — Analyzed industry white papers to give product development and strategy a solid benchmark.
  3. Clinical Trials & Technical Handover — Led clinical trials in collaboration with ITRI, verified product performance, and managed the technical handover that brought ITRI technologies in-house.
  4. App Development — Built engineering software for product testing and data analysis.
  5. Mechanical Design — Designed the form and structure of an infrared thermometer comb for pet health monitoring.
  6. Firmware Development — Refined and tuned demo code in C for product optimization.

Chapter Two · Semiconductor Equipment

Saultech Technology

In the Semiconductor Development Department, I helped drive the company's transformation:

  1. Product Owner — CL10 — Owned the world's first semiconductor chip cleaning machine: mechanism and process-layout design, module verification, invention patents, and cross-department coordination to bring the machine to completion.
  2. Infrared Detection Module — IN10 — Led the design, assembly, power distribution, testing and tuning of a bonding-inspection module that reads wafers with infrared light, selecting the optics myself.
  3. Particle Detection System — Designed an optical path pairing a laser with a photomultiplier tube (PMT) to detect particles with high precision.
  4. Motion Animation — Independently created the die-bonder motion animations shown at the company's semiconductor exhibition booth.
  5. Calibration & Patent Documentation — Completed calibration procedures and supported patent filings across multiple machines.
  6. Machine Design & Assembly — Modified and assembled other semiconductor machines to raise their performance.

Chapter Three · Motor Control & Drones — Present

Grenergy Technology

Today I bridge firmware and mechanical design in motor control development:

  1. Firmware Development — Motor control firmware built on zero-crossing detection, with matching driver programs — working primarily in CubeMX and Keil.
  2. Mechanical Design — Designing new motor mechanisms and maintaining existing ones; currently leading the mechanical design of a new drone project.
  3. Patent Management — Patent searches and portfolio maintenance.

Shifting from pure mechanical design into firmware was a bold stroke — but it is exactly this blend of disciplines that lets me build complete electromechanical solutions.
