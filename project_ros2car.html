LAB.01 / Mecanum AGV 全向輪 AGV

A compact AGV that thinks up top
and drives down below

A compact mecanum-wheel AGV built around a Jetson Orin Nano. Up top it plans, sees (YOLO · Gemini) and navigates; control commands drop to an STM32 driver board that drives brushed DC motors on mecanum wheels for full holonomic motion.
